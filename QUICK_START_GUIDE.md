# SafeNest Quick Start Guide
## How to Use SafeNest - Simple Step-by-Step Instructions

---

## 🚀 PART 1: FIRST-TIME SETUP

### For Seniors (Setting Up Your Phone)

#### Step 1: Install SafeNest App
1. Open **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
2. Search for **"SafeNest"**
3. Tap **Install**
4. Wait for download to complete

#### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. App opens to welcome screen
2. Choose **"I am a Senior"**
3. Enter your details:
   - Name (e.g., "John Smith")
   - Phone number
   - Email (optional)
4. Tap **"Create Account"**
5. App generates your **Household Code** (6 digits, like "ABC123")
6. **Write this code down** - give it to your caregiver

#### Step 3: Connect Your Smartwatch
1. Make sure your **Wear OS watch is paired** with your phone via Bluetooth
2. Open **Google Fit app** on your phone
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. In SafeNest app, tap ⚙️ **Settings** (bottom right)
5. Tap **"Connect Google Fit"**
6. Allow permissions when asked:
   - ✅ Read step count
   - ✅ Read heart rate
   - ✅ Read activity data
7. Tap **"Allow"**
8. You'll see **"Connected"** badge

#### Step 4: Share Your Code with Caregiver
1. In Settings, find **"Household Code"**
2. Show this code to your caregiver (e.g., "ABC123")
3. They will use this to connect to you

✅ **Setup Complete!** Now your vitals sync automatically.

---

### For Caregivers (Connecting to Senior)

#### Step 1: Install SafeNest App
1. Download SafeNest from Google Play Store
2. Install and open

#### Step 2: Create Caregiver Account
1. Choose **"I am a Caregiver"**
2. Enter your details:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Email
3. Tap **"Create Account"**

#### Step 3: Join Senior's Household
1. App asks: **"Enter Household Code"**
2. Type the code senior gave you (e.g., "ABC123")
3. Tap **"Join Household"**
4. You'll see senior's name appear
5. Tap **"Start Monitoring"**

✅ **Connected!** You can now see senior's health data.

## 📱 PART 2: DAILY USE - FOR SENIORS

### Morning Routine

#### 1. Open SafeNest App
- Tap app icon on home screen
- You'll see **Home** tab with your vitals

#### 2. Check Your Vitals Are Syncing
- Look for **Heart Rate** card
- Look for **Blood Oxygen** card
- If you see numbers → ✅ Watch is connected
- If you see "Not available" → Check if watch is on wrist

#### 3. Add Blood Pressure (If You Measure It)
1. Use your home blood pressure machine
2. Measure your BP (e.g., 120/80)
3. In SafeNest, tap 💓 **Vitals** (bottom menu)
4. Tap blue **"+ Add Vital Reading"** button
5. Choose **"Blood Pressure"** tab
6. Enter:
   - Systolic: **120**
   - Diastolic: **80**
7. Tap **"Save"**
8. Done! Your caregiver sees this immediately

#### 4. Take Your Medicines
1. When reminder pops up:
   ```
   🔔 Time for Aspirin (81mg)
   Morning dose - 8:00 AM
   ```
2. Take your medicine
3. Tap **"Mark as Taken"**
4. Medicine logged automatically

---

### Throughout the Day

#### Check Your Activity
1. Tap 🏠 **Home** tab
2. See your steps count (e.g., "3,245 steps")
3. See your heart rate (e.g., "72 bpm")
4. All updates automatically every 30 seconds

#### If You Need Help
1. Press the **big red SOS button** (center of Home screen)
2. Countdown starts: 10... 9... 8...
3. If accident, press **Cancel**
4. If emergency, let it reach 0
5. Your caregivers get instant alert with your location

---

### Evening Routine

#### 1. Add Weight (Once a Week)
1. Step on your scale
2. In SafeNest → 💓 **Vitals** tab
3. Tap **"+ Add Vital Reading"**
4. Choose **"Weight"** tab
5. Enter value (e.g., 75.2)
6. Choose unit: **kg** or **lbs**
7. Tap **"Save"**

#### 2. Check Messages from Caregiver
1. Tap 👥 **Caregivers** tab
2. See if any messages
3. Tap to call or message back

#### 3. Charge Your Devices
- Put phone on charger
- Put watch on charger
- This ensures overnight monitoring works

## 📱 PART 3: DAILY USE - FOR CAREGIVERS

### Morning Check (5 minutes)

#### 1. Open SafeNest App
- Tap app icon
- You'll see **Caregiver Dashboard**

#### 2. Check Senior Status (Home Tab)
Look at top section:
```
[Senior Name]
Status: ✅ Normal
Battery: 85%
Last seen: 2 min ago
Location: Home
```

Check vitals summary:
```
❤️ Heart Rate: 72 bpm - Normal
🫁 Blood Oxygen: 98% - Good  
👟 Steps: 1,245 today
```

✅ If all green → Senior is safe
⚠️ If red warnings → Check further

#### 3. Check Latest Vitals (Vitals Tab)
1. Tap 💓 **Vitals** (4th tab from left)
2. See 6 vital cards:
   - Blood Pressure
   - Heart Rate
   - Temperature
   - Weight
   - Blood Sugar
   - Blood Oxygen
3. Check timestamps (e.g., "2h ago")
4. Look for status badges:
   - 🟢 Normal = Good
   - 🔴 Elevated/Fever = Needs attention

#### 4. Add Vitals for Senior (If Needed)
If senior can't measure themselves:
1. In **Vitals** tab
2. Tap **"Add Vital Reading"** (bottom button)
3. Choose vital type
4. Enter measurement
5. Tap Save
6. Data appears immediately

---

### Weekly Review (10 minutes)

#### 1. Check Compliance Tab
1. Tap 📊 **Compliance** (5th tab)
2. See medicine compliance:
   ```
   Overall: 85%
   34 taken • 4 missed • 2 skipped
   ```
3. Check if senior is taking medicines regularly

#### 2. View Vitals Graphs
Scroll down in Compliance tab to see:

**5 Separate Graphs:**
- 📊 Blood Pressure (7-day or 30-day view)
- ❤️ Heart Rate trend
- 🌡️ Temperature pattern
- ⚖️ Weight changes
- 🩸 Blood Sugar levels

**How to Use:**
1. Tap **"7 Days"** or **"30 Days"** button
2. Look for trends:
   - ↗️ Rising BP = Concern
   - ↘️ Dropping weight = Concern
   - → Stable = Good

#### 3. Generate Health Analysis
1. Still in **Compliance** tab
2. Scroll to bottom
3. Tap **"Generate Report & Analysis"** button
4. Modal opens showing:

**Overall Health Risk:**
```
65/100
⚠️ Moderate Risk
Trend: Declining
```

**Risk Breakdown:**
```
Cardiovascular: 72/100 (High BP)
Metabolic: 35/100 (Good)
Medication Compliance: 45/100 (Missing doses)
```

**Health Alerts:**
```
🚨 Hypertension Risk: 85% probability
   Last 7 BP readings >140/90
   💡 Recommendation: Schedule doctor visit within 3 days
```

5. Read all predictions
6. Take action based on severity:
   - 🚨 High Risk → Call doctor immediately
   - ⚠️ Medium Risk → Monitor closely
   - ℹ️ Low Risk → Keep watching

### When You Get an Alert

#### Emergency Alert (SOS)
```
🚨 EMERGENCY ALERT
John Smith pressed SOS button
Location: 123 Main St, Springfield
Distance: 2.5 km from you
```

**What to Do:**
1. Tap notification
2. App opens to senior's location map
3. Tap **"Call Senior"** button
4. If no answer → Tap **"Directions"**
5. Drive to location
6. If serious → Call 911 first

#### Fall Detection Alert
```
⚠️ FALL DETECTED
John Smith may have fallen
Location: Home (Living Room)
```

**What to Do:**
1. Tap notification
2. Tap **"Call Senior"** button
3. Ask: "Are you okay? Did you fall?"
4. If injured → Call ambulance
5. If false alarm → Senior taps "I'm Okay" in their app

#### Medicine Missed Alert
```
💊 Medicine Missed
John Smith missed Aspirin (8:00 AM)
3 consecutive misses this week
```

**What to Do:**
1. Call senior
2. Remind them to take medicine
3. Check if they're feeling unwell
4. Check compliance tab for patterns

## 📊 PART 5: UNDERSTANDING THE DATA

### Vitals Reference Values

#### Blood Pressure
- 🟢 **Normal**: <120/80 mmHg
- 🟡 **Elevated**: 120-129/<80 mmHg
- 🔴 **High**: ≥140/90 mmHg
- **When to worry**: >140/90 for multiple days

#### Heart Rate
- 🟢 **Normal**: 60-100 bpm (resting)
- 🟡 **Bradycardia**: <60 bpm (slow)
- 🔴 **Tachycardia**: >100 bpm (fast)
- **When to worry**: Consistently <50 or >120

#### Blood Oxygen (SpO2)
- 🟢 **Good**: ≥95%
- 🟡 **Low**: 90-94%
- 🔴 **Critical**: <90%
- **When to worry**: <92% for extended time

#### Temperature
- 🟢 **Normal**: 97-99°F (36.1-37.2°C)
- 🟡 **Low Fever**: 99-100.3°F
- 🔴 **Fever**: >100.4°F (38°C)
- **When to worry**: >100.4°F for 3+ days

#### Blood Sugar (for diabetics)
- 🟢 **Normal**: 70-130 mg/dL (fasting)
- 🟡 **High**: 130-180 mg/dL
- 🔴 **Very High**: >180 mg/dL
- **When to worry**: <70 (hypoglycemia) or >180 frequently

## ⚠️ PART 6: TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: Watch Not Syncing

**Check:**
1. Is watch on senior's wrist? (must be snug)
2. Is watch charged? (needs >20% battery)
3. Is Bluetooth on? (Settings → Bluetooth → ON)
4. Is Google Fit app installed on phone?
5. Did senior grant permissions?

**Fix:**
1. Open Google Fit app
2. Verify watch shows in app
3. In SafeNest → Settings → Disconnect Google Fit
4. Then reconnect (grant permissions again)
5. Wait 30 seconds for sync

---

### Problem: No Heart Rate Data

**Common Causes:**
- Watch too loose on wrist
- Watch battery dead
- Senior took watch off
- Hair/tattoos blocking sensor

**Fix:**
1. Tighten watch band (should be snug but comfortable)
2. Clean watch sensor (back of watch)
3. Try other wrist
4. Wait 5 minutes for reading

---

### Problem: Caregiver Not Receiving Alerts

**Check:**
1. Phone notifications enabled for SafeNest?
   - Android Settings → Apps → SafeNest → Notifications → ON
2. Phone not in Do Not Disturb mode?
3. Internet connection working?

**Fix:**
1. Enable all notification permissions
2. Disable battery optimization for SafeNest
3. Restart app

---

### Problem: Wrong Household Code

**Fix for Senior:**
1. Settings → View Household Code
2. Share correct code with caregiver

**Fix for Caregiver:**
1. Settings → Leave Household
2. Enter correct code
3. Join again

## 📅 PART 8: BEST PRACTICES

### For Seniors:
✅ **Wear watch 24/7** (even sleeping - tracks sleep quality)
✅ **Charge devices nightly** (phone + watch)
✅ **Add vitals at same time daily** (consistency helps caregivers spot trends)
✅ **Keep phone nearby** (for fall detection)
✅ **Enable location sharing** (helps in emergencies)
✅ **Test SOS button weekly** (press and cancel to practice)
✅ **Update caregivers on changes** (new medicines, doctor visits)

### For Caregivers:
✅ **Check app daily** (5-minute morning review)
✅ **Review weekly graphs** (spot trends early)
✅ **Generate report monthly** (15-day minimum data)
✅ **Call senior regularly** (not just when alerts come)
✅ **Document concerns** (share with doctor)
✅ **Update emergency contacts** (keep phone numbers current)
✅ **Test alert system** (ask senior to test SOS)
